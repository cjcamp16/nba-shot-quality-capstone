## Final Research Questions

We began the project with three research questions in our project plan, and after developing the methodology and the model we carried all three through the full analysis without dropping or merging any of them. Each one is answered in the Analysis section. The three final research questions are:

1. **Which players and teams consistently outperform their expected shot value, and what distinguishes them?**

2. **How has shot success by court location changed across the 2013-present tracking era?**

3. **Does shot-quality-adjusted player ranking differ noticeably from traditional efficiency metrics (FG%, eFG%, TS%)?**

The only refinement we made over the course of the project was to the data each question draws on rather than to the questions themselves. The shot-quality model behind RQ1 and RQ3 is built on the single season (2014-15) for which per-shot tracking features are publicly available, while RQ2, which is about change over time, uses the full thirteen seasons of location data. The questions we set out to answer are the questions we answered.
