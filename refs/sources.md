# Sources

Running list of everything we're using for the lit review, methodology, and ethics sections. Updated as we add things.

## Downloaded — sitting in this folder

| File | What it is | Why it matters | Where it came from |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cervone_2014_pointwise.pdf` | Cervone, D'Amour, Bornn & Goldsberry (2014). *POINTWISE: Predicting Points and Valuing Decisions in Real Time with NBA Optical Tracking Data.* MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference. | The seminal expected-possession-value paper. Anchors the methodology and lit review. | lukebornn.com/papers/cervone_ssac_2014.pdf |
| `barocas_hardt_narayanan_fairmlbook.pdf` | Barocas, Hardt & Narayanan (2023). *Fairness and Machine Learning: Limitations and Opportunities.* MIT Press. | Foundation for the ethics section. Free under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. | fairmlbook.org/pdf/fairmlbook.pdf |

## Need to acquire (copyrighted books)

| Source | Why it matters | How to get it |
|---|---|---|
| Goldsberry, K. (2019). *SprawlBall: A Visual Tour of the New Era of the NBA.* Houghton Mifflin Harcourt. | The most accessible book-length treatment of modern shot-selection analytics. Highly readable, great for framing. | Purchase or borrow from the ASU library |
| Oliver, D. (2004). *Basketball on Paper: Rules and Tools for Performance Analysis.* Brassey's. | The foundational basketball analytics text. Good for methodology vocabulary (Four Factors, possessions, etc.). | Purchase or borrow from the ASU library |

## Conventions

- Save PDFs here with the same naming style: `lastname_year_keyword.pdf`
- When you cite something in `docs/`, also add the citation to `docs/09_references.md`
- Don't commit PDFs larger than ~20 MB — link to them instead
- Books we can't legally share stay in this list as "need to acquire," not as committed files
